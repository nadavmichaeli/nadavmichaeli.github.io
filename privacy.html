Privacy policy

Content Factory · last updated 19 October 2026

What the app is

The Content Factory app is an internal reporting tool. It is used only by the owner of the Content Factory channels to read results for those channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data it reads

  • From Google (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, read-only scopes): channel name, subscriber counts, and per-video statistics and analytics (views, watch time, audience retention, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained) for channels the owner manages.
  • From Instagram (Instagram API, read-only insights): the same kind of per-post results for the owner's own Instagram accounts.

It does not read email, contacts, files, or any data about other people's accounts, and it cannot post, edit or delete anything.

How the data is used and kept

The numbers are stored on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private records, and are used only to compare the channels' videos. They are not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Content Factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access tokens

Sign-in tokens are kept in an owner-only folder on that computer. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Instagram's Apps and websites settings.
